---
title: "User Management"
description: "Manage workspace members, roles, and app-level permissions."
---

Appsmith's permission model has two levels: **workspace roles** that control what a member can do across the entire workspace, and **app-level sharing** that lets you give specific people access to individual apps.

<Note>
  Appsmith Cloud has user limits based on your plan tier. Self-hosted instances on the Community Edition have no user limit, but some features (user groups, audit logs, SSO) require a Business or Enterprise license.
</Note>

## Workspace roles

Every member of a workspace is assigned one of three roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Administrator** | Manage members, roles, datasources, and all apps in the workspace. Full access to Admin Settings. |
| **Developer** | Create and edit apps, create and modify datasources, invite viewers. Cannot manage workspace members or access Admin Settings. |
| **Viewer** | View and use published apps. Cannot edit apps or access the editor. |

Roles apply across the entire workspace. To give someone narrower access — for example, edit access to one app but view-only on others — use app-level sharing instead.

## Inviting users to a workspace

1. From the workspace home screen, click **Share** (top-right).
2. Enter the user's email address.
3. Select a role: **Administrator**, **Developer**, or **Viewer**.
4. Click **Invite**.

The invited user receives an email with a link to accept the invitation. If `APPSMITH_SIGNUP_DISABLED` is set on your self-hosted instance, only users who receive an invitation can create an account. See [Custom Authentication](/guides/custom-authentication) for signup control options.

## App-level sharing

Share a specific app without changing the user's workspace role:

1. Open the app in the editor.
2. Click **Share** in the top-right toolbar.
3. Enter the user's email and select **Can view** or **Can edit**.
4. Click **Invite**.

Users with app-level access can only see apps they have been explicitly invited to, even if they are workspace members with a Viewer role.

To make an app publicly accessible (no login required):

1. Open **Share**.
2. Toggle **Make the application public**.

Public apps are viewable by anyone with the URL without an Appsmith account.

## Managing user groups (Enterprise)

On Enterprise plans, you can create user groups to assign roles and app access in bulk:

1. Go to **Admin Settings** → **Groups**.
2. Click **Create group** and give it a name (e.g., `Support Team`).
3. Add members to the group by email.
4. Assign the group a workspace role or grant it access to specific apps.

Changes to a group's permissions propagate to all members immediately. This is the recommended approach for managing access for teams of five or more users.

## Removing users

**From a workspace:**

1. Go to **Workspace Settings** → **Members**.
2. Find the user and click the role dropdown next to their name.
3. Select **Remove user**.

The user loses access to all apps in the workspace immediately.

**Revoking app-level access:**

1. Open the app's **Share** dialog.
2. Find the user in the list and click **Remove**.
