title: "Quickstart"

---

This guide walks you through building a simple app that reads from a database and displays the results in a table. You'll connect a datasource, write a query, bind data to a widget, and publish.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Appsmith Cloud">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign up">
        Go to [app.appsmith.com](https://app.appsmith.com) and create a free account. No credit card required.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create an app">
        From your workspace dashboard, click **New** and select **Application**. Give it a name and click **Create**.

        You land in the Appsmith editor with a blank canvas.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect a datasource">
        In the left sidebar, click **Datasources** → **+ New datasource**. Select your database type (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, and others are supported) and enter your connection credentials.

        Click **Test** to verify the connection, then **Save**.

        <Note>
          To get started without your own database, use the built-in sample datasource. Click **+ New datasource** → **Sample databases** → **users**.
        </Note>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Write a query">
        With your datasource selected, click **+ New query**. In the query editor, enter:

        ```sql
        SELECT * FROM users LIMIT 10;
        ```

        Click **Run**. Results appear in the response pane below the editor.

        Rename the query `getUsersQuery` using the name field at the top of the editor.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Bind data to a Table widget">
        Go back to the canvas. In the widget panel on the left, drag a **Table** widget onto the canvas.

        In the property pane on the right, find the **Table data** field and enter:

        ```
        {{ getUsersQuery.data }}
        ```

        The table populates with your query results immediately.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Publish your app">
        Click **Deploy** in the top-right corner. Appsmith builds and publishes your app. Share the generated URL with your team.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Self-hosted">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Install Appsmith with Docker">
        Make sure Docker and Docker Compose are installed, then run:

        ```bash
        curl -L https://bit.ly/32jBNin -o docker-compose.yml
        docker compose up -d
        ```

        Appsmith starts on port 80. Open `http://localhost` in your browser.

        <Tip>
          See [Cloud vs. Self-Hosted](/getting-started/cloud-vs-self-hosted) for a full comparison, including Kubernetes and AWS deployment options.
        </Tip>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create an admin account">
        On first launch, you are prompted to create an admin account. Enter your name, email, and password, then click **Setup**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create an app">
        From your workspace dashboard, click **New** → **Application**. Give it a name and click **Create**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect a datasource">
        In the left sidebar, click **Datasources** → **+ New datasource**. Select your database type and enter connection details.

        Click **Test**, then **Save**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Write a query">
        With your datasource open, click **+ New query** and enter:

        ```sql
        SELECT * FROM users LIMIT 10;
        ```

        Click **Run** to verify results, then rename the query `getUsersQuery`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Bind data to a Table widget">
        Drag a **Table** widget onto the canvas. In the property pane, set **Table data** to:

        ```
        {{ getUsersQuery.data }}
        ```

        The table fills with your query results.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Publish your app">
        Click **Deploy**. Your app is live and accessible to anyone with the link or viewer access.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
