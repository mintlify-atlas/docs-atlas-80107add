## What you can build

Appsmith is used by thousands of teams to build tools like:

- **Admin panels** — Manage users, orders, and content with full CRUD operations
- **Dashboards** — Visualize metrics from databases, APIs, and spreadsheets
- **Customer portals** — Let customers self-serve tickets, orders, and account info
- **Approval workflows** — Route requests through multi-step approval chains
- **Data pipelines** — Trigger and monitor ETL jobs and scheduled tasks

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your data">
    Add a datasource — PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, REST API, Google Sheets, and more. Credentials are stored securely and never exposed to the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the UI">
    Drag widgets onto the canvas: tables, forms, charts, buttons, text inputs. Every widget property can be bound to live query data using `{{ }}` syntax.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write queries and logic">
    Write SQL, NoSQL queries, or API calls in the query editor. Use JavaScript to transform data, handle events, and chain actions together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and share">
    Publish your app with one click. Share it with your team via link, manage access with roles, or embed it in your existing tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>
